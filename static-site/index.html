About the practice

Darkes Architecture is a creative practice with wide experience of residential projects. We turn your vision into reality with innovative and sustainable design.

We have worked in architectural practice in North Wales for over 30 years. We design home extensions, renovations, loft conversions, alterations and new-build homes. We work closely with each client, so every project reflects the character and charm of the area.

Our services

We can be with you every step of the way, from the first concept to completion.

  • Extensions, alterations and loft conversions

    Whether you want more living space or a new layout for the rooms you have, we design for your needs and the way you live.

  • Renovations

    We modernise older homes and bring tired spaces back to life, with style and function.

  • New-build homes

    Starting from scratch? We will help you design a home that reflects your taste and suits its surroundings.

  • Planning permission and building regulations

    We prepare and submit planning applications and building regulations applications to get the approvals you need.

  • Technical drawings and specifications

    We produce detailed technical drawings and specifications, so builders can give you accurate quotes.
